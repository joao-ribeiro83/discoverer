# Settings

Customize your language and theme preferences in Discoverer Neo.

## Accessing Settings

You can reach the Settings page in two ways:

### From the Sidebar

1. In the left sidebar, scroll to the bottom
2. Click **Settings** (icon: gear)

### From the Profile Menu

1. In the top-right header, click your email or profile icon
2. Select **Settings** from the dropdown menu

The Settings page loads and displays your current language and theme choices.

## Language Preferences

Choose the language for the entire Discoverer Neo user interface.

### Changing Language

1. On the Settings page, look for the **Language** section
2. Click the language dropdown menu
3. Select your preferred language:
   - **English** — Default language
   - **Português (Portugal)** — European Portuguese
   - **Français (France)** — French (France)
   - **Español (España)** — Spanish (Spain)

The interface updates immediately — all menus, labels, buttons, and page content switch to your selected language.

### Saving Your Choice

The language change is just a **preview** until you click **Save** at the bottom of the page. If you reload the page or log in elsewhere before saving, the interface reverts to your last-saved language preference.

## Theme Preferences

Choose a visual theme that works best for you: Light, Dark, or High-Contrast.

### Viewing Theme Options

The Settings page displays three clickable theme swatches, each showing a preview of what the interface looks like in that theme:

- **Light** — Traditional light background with dark text (good for bright environments)
- **Dark** — Dark background with light text (reduces eye strain in low-light settings)
- **High-Contrast** — Enhanced contrast colors for improved readability and accessibility

### Changing Theme

1. On the Settings page, look for the **Theme** section
2. Click on the theme swatch you want to try
3. The entire interface immediately switches to that theme so you can preview it

A checkmark appears on the selected theme.

### Saving Your Choice

Like language changes, theme changes are a **preview** until you click **Save** at the bottom of the page. If you reload or navigate away without saving, the interface reverts to your last-saved theme preference.

## Saving Your Preferences

Once you've chosen your preferred language and theme:

1. Review your choices at the top of the Settings page
2. Click **Save** at the bottom
3. A confirmation message appears: "Settings saved"

Your preferences are now stored with your account. The next time you log in (from any device or browser), Discoverer Neo loads with your saved language and theme automatically.

## Tips

- **Live Preview:** Feel free to experiment! Language and theme changes apply immediately so you can see what you're choosing before saving.
- **No Admin Required:** You manage your own preferences — there's no administrator approval needed.
- **Per-User Settings:** Each user account has its own language and theme. Other users' preferences don't affect yours.
- **Persistent:** Your saved preferences follow you across devices and browsers as long as you're logged into the same account.

## Troubleshooting

### Changes Don't Persist After Reload

**Cause:** You selected a new language or theme but didn't click **Save**.

**Solution:** Go back to Settings, choose your preferences again, and click **Save** before leaving the page.

### Settings Page Won't Load

**Cause:** Network connection issue or API error.

**Solution:** 
1. Try refreshing the page
2. Check your internet connection
3. If the problem persists, contact your administrator

### Theme Colors Look Wrong

**Cause:** Browser cache or CSS loading issue.

**Solution:**
1. Hard refresh your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows/Linux, Cmd+Shift+R on Mac)
2. Clear your browser cache
3. Try a different browser to test
