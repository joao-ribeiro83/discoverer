# Your role in one page

You have the **VIEWER** role. Your main job is to open maps and read their results. A **map** is a report. A **business area** is a group of related data.

| You can | You cannot |
|---|---|
| See your own, public and shared maps | See other people's private maps |
| Run maps, sort, filter and drill down | Copy a map or workbook |
| Export, if the map is public or shared as **Can export** or **Can edit** | Export a map shared as **Can view** |
| Change your language and theme | Open administration pages |

Share levels: **Can view** = run only. **Can export** = run, export, schedule. **Can edit** = all of that plus change the map. The share, not your role, decides this.

Sign in with **Email** and **Password**, then **Sign in**. A temporary password must be changed first (at least 12 characters). To leave, click your name, then **Log out**.

# Dashboard and Maps

**Dashboard** is a read-only summary. **Maps** lists what you can open.

![The Maps list, All tab, where each map has only the Open icon.](shots/en/viewer/03-maps-all.png)

1. Click **Maps** in the sidebar.
2. Open **Shared with me** or **All**.
3. Search by name or pick a **Business Area**.
4. Click the map name or the eye icon.

| Choice | Line |
|---|---|
| **Mine** / **Shared with me** / **All** | Your own, shared, or everything you can see |
| Copy | Not available for your role |
| Other icons | Work only if you own the map or hold **Can edit** or **Can export** |

# Run and read a map

The viewer runs a map and shows its rows.

![A completed run with Excel, CSV and PDF buttons over the results grid.](shots/en/viewer/06-viewer-results.png)

1. Click **Run**. Fill in **Run parameters** if asked.
2. Read the rows. Click a header to sort. Type in **Filter…** to narrow.
3. Double-click a row for **Drill to Detail**.
4. Click **Load more** if not all rows are shown.
5. Click **Run again** for fresh data. A result stays valid for 24 hours.

| Choice | Line |
|---|---|
| **Excel**, **CSV**, **PDF** | Export. Only with **Can export** or **Can edit**, or on a public map. Otherwise "Forbidden" |
| **Not entitled to run** | No access to the data. Ask your administrator |
| **Cancel** | Only while the run is queued |

# Runs and Exports

**Runs** lists your runs. **Exports** lists your files.

![The Runs page listing the Viewer's own runs.](shots/en/viewer/07-runs.png)

1. Open **Runs** to see status and **Expires in**.
2. Click **Open** to see a stored result, or **Run again**.
3. Open **Exports** and click **Download** on a finished file.

| Choice | Line |
|---|---|
| **Delete** | Removes a run for good |
| Files | Kept 7 days |

# Schedules

Schedules run a map by themselves. You can create one only for a map you own or that is shared as **Can export** or **Can edit**. Public maps and **Can view** shares cannot be scheduled.

![The Schedules page with the message No schedules yet.](shots/en/viewer/09-schedules.png)

1. Click **New Schedule**, or the calendar icon on **Maps**.
2. Choose the **Map** and a **Name**.
3. Choose **Frequency**, **Timezone** and **Output Format**.
4. Click **Save**.

| Choice | Line |
|---|---|
| **Frequency** | **Daily (midnight)**, **Weekly (Sunday, midnight)**, **Monthly (1st, midnight)**, **Custom** |
| **Output Format** | **Excel (.xlsx)** or **CSV** |

# Settings

Open **Settings** from the sidebar.

![The Settings page with the Language, Theme and Color palette cards.](shots/en/common/04-settings.png)

1. Pick a **Display language**.
2. Pick **Light**, **Dark** or **High contrast**.
3. Pick a **Palette** (not with **High contrast**).
4. Click **Save**. Without it the choice stays on this browser only.
